Research

AI people can trust and act on.

Explainability runs through all of our work. We focus on four areas where a wrong prediction is costly and an unexplained one gets ignored.

  • Cybersecurity
  • Healthcare
  • Robotics & CPS
  • Computer Vision
  • Methods
01

Cybersecurity

Security teams can't act on alerts they don't understand. We build detection and response systems that explain their decisions, and we study how to secure AI systems themselves.

Focus areas

  • Explainable intrusion detectionDetectors that show why traffic was flagged, from rule extraction to self-organizing maps.
  • Agentic AI for incident responseSupervisable multi-agent systems that triage and respond to enterprise intrusions.
  • Cyber threat intelligenceGenerating, grounding, and checking threat intelligence with knowledge graphs and language models.
  • Securing AI itselfAttacks and defenses across the ML lifecycle, from MLOps pipelines to prompt-level controls for LLM agents.

02

Healthcare

Clinicians need predictions they can check. We connect patient data, medical knowledge, and language models to support decisions with evidence attached.

Focus areas

  • Patient-centric knowledge graphsConnected, structured views of patient data that support reasoning and decision support.
  • Clinical language modelsGrounding LLM answers in multiple trusted medical sources to produce patient-centric responses.
  • Interpretable risk predictionEarly warning from multivariate time series, such as sepsis onset, with explanations clinicians can verify.
  • Medical imaging and epidemiologyForecasting disease progression from MRI, and modeling how epidemics spread.

Selected papers

  • Patient-Centric Knowledge Graphs: A Survey of Current Methods, Challenges, and Applications2024 · Journal
  • MedInsight: A Multi-Source Context Augmentation Framework for Generating Patient-Centric Medical Responses using Large Language Models2024 · Journal
  • An Interpretable Approach to Prediction of Sepsis Using Multivariate Time Series2022 · Journal
  • A Novel Compartmental Model to Capture the Nonlinear Trend of COVID-192021 · Journal

03

Robotics & Cyber-Physical Systems

Machines in the field fail, drift, and get attacked. We predict failures before they happen, explain digital twins, and plan safe paths through rough terrain.

Focus areas

  • Predictive maintenanceRemaining-useful-life estimation and fault prediction that explains what is wearing out and why.
  • Digital twinsExplaining, and stress-testing, digital twins of vehicles and farms.
  • Autonomy in unstructured terrainUncertainty-aware path planning and multi-robot coverage for off-road and agricultural settings.
  • Security of robotic systemsThreats to AI-enabled robots, and training the workforce to counter them.

04

Computer Vision

Perception is where autonomy starts. We estimate where vehicles can drive, find hazards hidden in point clouds, and measure how realistic generated images are.

Focus areas

  • Traversability from aboveEstimating where ground vehicles can drive from aerial imagery.
  • 3D perceptionDetecting negative obstacles such as ditches and holes in LiDAR point clouds.
  • Generative model evaluationMeasuring the realism of synthetic images, starting with OCR.
  • Medical imagingTracking change in MRI over time.

Selected papers
