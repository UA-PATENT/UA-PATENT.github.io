Cybersecurity

Security teams can't act on alerts they don't understand. We build detection and response systems that explain their decisions, and we study how to secure AI systems themselves.

Focus areas

  • Explainable intrusion detectionDetectors that show why traffic was flagged, from rule extraction to self-organizing maps.
  • Agentic AI for incident responseSupervisable multi-agent systems that triage and respond to enterprise intrusions.
  • Cyber threat intelligenceGenerating, grounding, and checking threat intelligence with knowledge graphs and language models.
  • Securing AI itselfAttacks and defenses across the ML lifecycle, from MLOps pipelines to prompt-level controls for LLM agents.

Healthcare

Clinicians need predictions they can check. We connect patient data, medical knowledge, and language models to support decisions with evidence attached, while keeping patient data private.

Focus areas

  • Patient-centric knowledge graphsConnected, structured views of patient data that support reasoning and decision support.
  • Clinical language modelsGrounding LLM answers in trusted medical sources, and summarizing patient–doctor conversations.
  • Privacy-preserving medical imagingFederated, blockchain-backed deep learning that detects COVID-19 in CT scans without pooling patient data.
  • EpidemiologyModeling how epidemics spread, including the nonlinear trend of COVID-19.

Robotics & Cyber-Physical Systems

Machines in the field fail, drift, and get attacked. We predict failures before they happen, explain digital twins, secure robots, and build humanoid robots that interact safely with people.

Focus areas

  • Predictive maintenanceRemaining-useful-life estimation and fault prediction that explains what is wearing out and why.
  • Digital twinsExplaining, and stress-testing, digital twins of vehicles and farms.
  • Humanoid and bioinspired roboticsThe BRAINS Lab humanoid platform, with neurocognitive models of attention and memory for safe, adaptive behavior.
  • Autonomy in unstructured terrainPath planning and multi-robot coverage for off-road and agricultural settings.
  • Security of robotic systemsThreats to AI-enabled robots, and training the workforce to counter them.

Computer Vision

Perception is where autonomy starts. Much of this work happens in the BRAINS Lab: reading behavior from video, understanding surfaces by touch and sight, and analyzing medical, hyperspectral, and sports imagery.

Focus areas

  • Behavior and engagement from videoVision transformers that detect collaborative engagement and gaze in learning environments.
  • Visuo-tactile surface understandingClassifying and reconstructing surface materials from touch and sight, even from a single visible patch.
  • Medical and hyperspectral imagingDenoising and analyzing MRI, satellite multispectral, and hyperspectral images.
  • Sports analyticsPose-based analysis of pitching mechanics with the Alabama Baseball team.
  • Perception for autonomyCrack detection for autonomous navigation, and vision-language-guided robot navigation.

A lab within PATENT

BRAINS Lab

The Bioinspired Robotics, AI, Imaging & Neurocognitive Systems Laboratory builds intelligent, adaptive systems by combining AI, computer vision, image processing, data science, and cognitive neuroscience. Its goal is machines that move beyond today's AI toward more flexible, human-like intelligence.

Led by Dr. Noorbakhsh Amiri Golilarz, BRAINS works from a humanoid robot platform and collaborates across computer science, engineering, psychology, and medicine.
